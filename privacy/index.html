Privacy

Short version: your videos and projects stay on your computer.

The Cutkaar app

  • Your videos, photos, audio and projects are stored on your own computer. Cutkaar does not upload them.
  • Cutkaar has no account and no advertising, and it does not record what you edit or how you use it.
  • To look for updates, Cutkaar downloads a small file from this website and from GitHub. Each of these checks is counted as one app opening, together with the country and approximate city of your internet connection. Your IP address is not stored, and nothing in the count identifies you.
  • License check. When Cutkaar opens, it also tells this website a random ID that Cutkaar made for your computer and its version number, so the developer can count how many computers use Cutkaar and, if Cutkaar ever becomes paid, check licenses. The ID is not linked to your name, and no IP address is stored.
  • Online captions (optional). If you add your own Groq key and choose the online engine, the speech parts of your audio are sent to Groq to be turned into text. Groq's own privacy policy applies. Without a key, captions are made on your computer.
  • The offline speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face.

This website

  • No cookies and no tracking scripts. A setting for the language suggestion is kept only in your browser.
  • When the installer is downloaded, the download is counted with the country and approximate city of the connection, so the developer can see how many people use Cutkaar and where. IP addresses are not stored.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare Pages; fonts come from Google Fonts.
