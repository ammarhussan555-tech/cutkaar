A free CapCut alternative for PC

If you like editing with a CapCut-style timeline, Cutkaar will feel familiar. It is a free Windows editor made for long videos, captions in our own languages, and quick exports.

Who it is for

  • YouTubers who cut long recordings into short clips.
  • Anyone who needs Urdu or Hindi captions without typing them.
  • People who want their videos to stay on their own computer.
